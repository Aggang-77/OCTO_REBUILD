# XPBD centerline bending update — 2026-11-15

Previous bend solver used an iteration-local `-C/(Σw+α/h²)` positional correction and discarded its multiplier. It now stores a three-component multiplier per inner rod node and solves `Δλ = (-C - (α/h²)λ)/(w_left + 4w_center + w_right + α/h²)` for the positional curvature proxy `C = x_left - 2x_center + x_right - target`. The accumulated multiplier resets once per actual `1/240 s` substep, like distance, volume and material-roll constraints. This removes a particular iteration-dependent approximation; it does **not** give the rod a full Cosserat orientation/shear constitutive law.

Validation: MSVC Release build and CTest **2/2 PASS**. A fixture with identical bend targets at four versus sixteen constraint sweeps produced tip positions within 0.08 m after 240 ticks; this broad bound detects major differences and does not establish convergence or biological accuracy. The two-animal 10,000-tick soak passed. `bend_xpbd_10.csv` records 10 animals with active bend/stretch/twist (600 measured ticks after 120 warmup): physics mean **4.48 ms**, p95 **4.86 ms**, no reported nonfinite values. These are native-only times.

Actual Godot 4.6.2 GPU scene with 10 original GameReady meshes reached tick 60, screenshot `bend_xpbd_10.png`; 59 ticks took **1557 ms**, or **37.9 simulation ticks/s**. The distinct two-animal test mode `--test-actuation --screenshot=../docs/validation/actuated_2.png` drives one arm through the C++ command path; at tick 60 it reported material roll **1.35 rad** and the captured arm appears bent. This test checks transport and observed rendering, not skin-to-collider geometric error. The physics contact shape remains a radius proxy; orientation/bending/shear coupling, torso torque reaction, elliptical cross-section and complete 10-animal 60 Hz are still open.
