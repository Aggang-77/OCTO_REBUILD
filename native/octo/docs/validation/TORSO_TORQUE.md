# Torso reaction from material twist — 2026-11-15

Previously, the root roll was fixed at zero, so an active twist constraint could rotate the arm's material frames without applying an opposite torque to the body. The first segment's XPBD torsion constraint now uses the signed orientation of a deformable pair of torso nodes about the arm's tangent. Its derivative displaces the two body nodes in opposite directions with inverse-mass weighting while adjusting the first free roll angle. Remaining segment pairs retain the scalar XPBD relative-roll constraints. The root's rendered roll follows the resulting torso orientation. This is a small-angle local approximation: the arm axis is held fixed within one constraint evaluation, and the cage is a coarse seven-node model.

Verification in an isolated zero-gravity, no-floor, no-damping fixture: opposite signed twist commands for one arm over 120 ticks changed a torso node's position by **0.00114773 m** between the two runs. The positive run's last-tick angular momentum diagnostic (orbital particles plus material spin, about world origin) was **3.16643×10⁻⁹ kg·m²/s**, below the fixture acceptance bound `1×10⁻⁵ kg·m²/s`. This is not proof of exact momentum conservation for arbitrary body rotation, contact or changing tangent axes.

Two-animal **10,000-tick** soak PASS. `./build/release-final/octo_benchmark.exe 10 600 twist`: 10 active animals, 600 measured ticks after 120 warmup, mean **4.59 ms**, p95 **5.04 ms**, no reported nonfinite count; raw CSV `torso_torque_10.csv`. Godot 4.6.2 visible run: two-animal active input advanced 60 ticks and reported 1.41 rad material roll at arm midpoint; ten-animal passive display advanced 59 ticks in 1715 ms (**34.4 simulation ticks/s**). Interactive keyboard/mouse validation and complete GPU frame timing are still open.

Limitations: torsion remains one scalar rotation about a reconstructed tangent rather than independent quaternion orientation; bending and shear are not solved as coupled Cosserat constraints. The two torso nodes are not an anatomical mantle, and rotating section contact/torque against obstacles remains absent. Ten-animal 60 Hz and skin/collision agreement remain FAIL/NOT TESTED respectively. Next integrate shear and bending with 3D orientation/inertia and optimize the engine bridge/render path under a reproducible dense-contact scenario.
